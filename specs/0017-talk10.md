# 0017 — Conversation tuning of the ten-billion student → v7

Status: both arms failed G1 (2026-11-20): `talk10` (3e-5) benchmark 68, shelves +0.07–0.15; `talk10b` (1e-5) benchmark 66, shelves +0.03–0.06; no v7, `bil10/final` stays the Space default · Depends on: 0011, 0016 · Unblocks: v7, 0013

## 1. TL;DR
`bil10` (0016) read ten billion tokens of school text; in the quick benchmark its previews rose 59 → 61 → 66 of 100
(at 2.5B, 4B and 5.8B) and its final checkpoint scored 64: above v6 (62) but below `bil2-avg` (73), its start: more
knowledge, less of the answering form. We tune its result the way v6 was made (0011, `talk2`): **50M tokens**, every
other step a conversation, then compare with v6. If it keeps the shelves (G1) and answers better (G2), it is published
as **v7**. The first arm, `talk10` (3e-5), answered better (68) but lost on the shelves; `talk10b` repeated it at 1e-5:
less forgetting (+0.03–0.06), benchmark 66, still not within G1; no v7 from this spec.

## 2. Abstract
v6 is the 1B student after `talk2`: chat 55 → 117 of 160 from 50M tokens of conversations, with no loss on the shelves.
The same recipe now starts from a student that has read ~12x more (12.5B tokens against ~1B). One card is enough: 50M tokens are ~1.5 hours on
one GPU, so no DiLoCo.

## 3. Objective
- **Arm `talk10`** (`final_test.py`): from `checkpoints/final/bil10/final`, **50M tokens**; every other step request
  → reply in talk2's mix (teacher questions 30%, memory 20%, dialogue 20%, lesson, story, self, relation, unknown
  words, feelings), the other step the `bil10` shelves in `bil10`'s shares; learning rate 3e-5 after 100 steps, cosine
  to 3e-6; validation on the nine `bil10` shelves.
- **Arm `talk10b`**: the same, but learning rate 1e-5 (`bil10`'s last), cosine to 1e-6 (added after `talk10` failed G1).
- **Run** (`scripts/final/talk10.sh [TOKENS] [ARM]`, from the MacBook): wait for `bil10/final`, copy it to the data
  host (it has the shelves and `data/talk`), train the arm on one card there, copy `<arm>/final` to the Mac mini.
- **Compare** on the mini: quick benchmark (`scripts/hf/bench.py`) of v6, `bil10/final` and `<arm>/final`; then the
  spot check (`scripts/hf/spot_check.py`); bits per byte against v6 for information only (no gate).

**Out of scope:** new conversation data, honest answers (0013), other recipe changes than the two in §5.

## 4. Control Gates
| Gate | What | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Shelves kept | validation on every `bil10` shelf at most 0.02 above `bil10/final` |
| G2 | Answers | quick benchmark above v6 (62) and above `bil10/final`; spot check not worse than v6 (117 of 160) |
| G3 | Release | G1 and G2 → published as v7 (Space default, model repo); v6 stays on the Space beside it |

## 5. Additional Information
- **Why the same recipe:** `talk` at 1e-4 lifted shelf validation by 0.2–0.3 (0011); `talk2` at 3e-5 with half the
  steps on the shelves did not. Changing one thing at a time keeps the comparison with v6 clean.
- **If G1 fails:** a lower learning rate, starting at the one the run ended with (1e-5 for `bil10`) → `talk10b`.
- **If G2 fails:** a longer run (100M) or a higher share of teacher questions, one at a time.
- **Order:** G1 first; the spot check (G2) is run only for an arm that passed G1.

## 6. Results
- **`talk10`** (3e-5, 2026-11-19/20, 50M tokens, ~80 min on one card): quick benchmark 68 of 100 (v6 62, `bil10/final`
  64; knowledge 14 → 18, completing 13 → 14). **G1 failed:** validation above `bil10/final` on every shelf, edu 12
  3.353 → 3.491, textbooks 2.403 → 2.550, stories 2.404 → 2.555, dialogue 1.509 → 1.614, speech 3.173 → 3.256; +0.2
  within the first 3M tokens, then only partly back. `bil10` ended at 1e-5; 3e-5 with a fresh optimizer knocked it out
  of its minimum, as `talk` at 1e-4 did after the 1B run (0011). No spot check: G1 had already failed.
- **`talk10b`** (1e-5, cosine to 1e-6, 2026-11-20, 50M tokens, ~85 min): quick benchmark 66 of 100 (v6 62, `bil10/final`
  64; yes/no 12 → 13, knowledge 14 → 16, completing 13 → 12). **G1 failed, by less:** at 48M tokens validation above
  `bil10/final` on every shelf by +0.03–0.06 (speech 3.173 → 3.208, stories 1.430 → 1.458, dialogue 1.509 → 1.540,
  edu 12 3.353 → 3.412, textbooks 2.403 → 2.460, stories (s4) 2.404 → 2.464); +0.03–0.05 at 3M already, then flat. No
  spot check (G1 had failed).
- **Reading:** half the steps are conversations, so some loss on school text is the price of the answering form;
  the lower learning rate halved it but did not remove it, and bought 2 benchmark points instead of 4.
